# r/boston launch post

## ⚠️ Read this first: r/boston Rule 5

> *"No self/link posts that constitute advertising — no crowdfunding, coupons,
> referral programs, self-affiliated business, event, or service promoting…
> Habitually linking to one's personal blog … and text-based promotional
> content is discouraged."*

A "check out the site I built" post is very likely to be removed under this
rule, even though Pour Finder is free and non-commercial. Rule 6 also bans
posts about services, and Rule 8 bans low-effort posts.

**So the strategy below is not "post a link."** It's: *give the research away as
the post*. A list of every cheap beer in Boston with sources is exactly the kind
of thing r/boston upvotes. The site becomes a footnote people can opt into,
not the pitch.

**Do this in order:**

1. **Message the mods first** (draft below). Takes two minutes, and it's the
   difference between a post that stays up and a shadow-removal you never see.
2. If they say yes → post **Option A** with the link.
3. If they say no or don't reply → post **Option A without the link**. The list
   still stands on its own, you still get the crowdsourced replies you actually
   want, and you can DM the site to anyone who asks.

Post **Thursday or Friday, late afternoon** — that's when people are actually
thinking about where to drink.

## 2. Option A — the post (recommended)

**Title:**

```
I made a list of every cheap beer I could find in Boston/Cambridge/Somerville. What am I missing?
```

**Body:**

```
I got annoyed at not knowing where a cheap beer actually was, so I started
writing them down. Here's everything I've confirmed so far. Prices are what
the menu says — no tax, no tip.

**Under $3**
- Coogan's (171 Milk St) — $1 Bud draft, $1 Bud Light draft. Size isn't listed
  and it's not a big pour, but it's a dollar.
- Sissy K's (6 Commercial St) — $2 beer, advertised all day every day. They
  don't say which beer.
- SideBar (14 Bromfield St) — $2 beer reported. I haven't confirmed which one.
- Biddy Early's (141 Pearl St) — $3 PBR.
- State Park (1 Kendall Sq, Cambridge) — Genesee around $3.

**$4–6**
- Olde Magoun's Saloon (518 Medford St, Somerville) — $4 Narragansett tallboy.
- J.J. Donovan's — Miller around $4–5 depending on when you go.
- Boston Burger Co (1100 Boylston) — $10 PBR pitcher, and it's a big pitcher.
- Cambridge Common (1667 Mass Ave) — $4.95 Bud/Bud Light/Miller Lite/High Life,
  $5.95 Narragansett 16oz.
- Lower Mills Tavern (2269 Dorchester Ave) — $5 High Life, Bud Light, Bud,
  Miller Lite. $6 Coors Light, Corona, Mich Ultra, Modelo.
- Sullivan's Tap (168 Canal St) — $6 Sully's Light draft, $6 Narragansett.

**Worth knowing**
- Redbones in Davis had $5 pints and $10 liters, but it changed ownership, so
  I'd treat those as unconfirmed until someone checks.
- Mike's in Davis lists sizes only as "medium" and "large" — medium Coors is
  $9.50, large is $12.50, so read the menu carefully.
- A&B Kitchen's $3/22oz Coors Light ended July 31.

**What I'm after:** the ones I've obviously missed. Dive bars, happy hours,
neighborhoods I never drink in — Eastie, Southie, JP, Allston, Dorchester,
Roslindale. If you know one, drop it below with the bar and the price. If you
know the pour size, even better; if you don't, say so rather than guessing,
because "$1 draft, size unknown" is still useful and a made-up number isn't.

Also happy to be told I'm wrong about any of these — some are secondhand and
prices move.
```

**If the mods approved a link**, add this at the very end:

```
(I've been keeping all of this on a little site so it stays current —
pour-finder.vercel.app. It's free, no ads, no account, and you can tap
"still there" or "gone" on anything. Mods okayed the link.)
```

## 5. Before you post

- [ ] The site actually has the deals in it (`npm run setup:prod`) — a link to
      an empty site is worse than no link.
- [ ] Open it on your phone first. Most of the traffic will be mobile.
- [ ] Modmail sent and answered.
- [ ] Be around for the first two hours to reply. Early comments decide whether
      a thread lives.
